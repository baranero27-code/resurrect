## Post 2 · the mechanism (thread)
1/ Every memecoin launch today has the same bug: there is a "first".
First block, first buyer, first bundle. The launch is decided before a human can click.

2/ Resurrect has no first.
A coin opens as a 15-minute sealed batch. You bid. Nobody sees it — not us, not bots, not the dev. Only a hash goes on chain.

3/ When the batch closes, every bid is revealed and summed, then divided by the 500M tokens on sale.
That number is the price. For everyone. Second 1 and second 899 pay the same.

4/ The pool opens at exactly that price, seeded with everything raised + the other 50% of supply — it opens at exactly the price you paid.
It belongs to the program. There is no instruction that can withdraw it. Not for the dev. Not for us.

5/ No hidden allocation. In Auction mode the dev bids, sealed, like you. In Open mode the dev may make one buy at the opening price — capped at 20% and stamped on the coin page as DEV BOUGHT x%. In daylight or not at all.

6/ 1% of every trade goes back to holders, paid in the asset the coin is paired with. Pair with SOL, earn SOL. Pair with a tokenized stock, earn the stock.

7/ Open mode has a protected start the creator cannot switch off: 90 s at one price, 1.5% per wallet for 5 min, a 5%→1% fee whose surcharge goes to holders.
Code is public. Audit package is public.
