HOW IT WORKS four rules, enforced by contract

Rx 01

Sealed batch auction

Fifteen minutes. Bids hidden until close. One clearing price for every participant.

removes: sniper bots
Rx 02

One human, one wallet

Proof-of-personhood gates every bid and trade. Sybil wallets cost a verification each, then a ban.

removes: bundlers · wash volume
Rx 03

Immutable liquidity

LP seeds and locks at graduation. No admin key, no pull function, no upgrade path.

removes: rug pulls
Rx 04

Zero dev allocation

No pre-mint, no team wallet, no first buy. The creator bids in the batch like everyone else.

removes: insider dumps
