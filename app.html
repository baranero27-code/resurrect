HOW OPEN MODE WORKS

1 · createone transaction: mint, revoke authority, whole supply onto a constant-product curve with the virtual depth you set. Trading starts immediately.
2 · protected startfirst 90 s: every buy and sell at the opening price, so being fast earns nothing. First 5 min: max 1.5% of supply per wallet; the creator gets at most one labelled dev buy (≤20%) inside the first 90 s and nothing else until the cap ends. Fee starts at 5% and falls to 1% over 15 min; the extra goes to holders. No sell lock — you can always exit.
3 · tradeafter that it is a normal curve: buy and sell any size, 1% fee in the pairing asset.
4 · venueResurrect pool: stays on the curve forever. Raydium: when the real reserve reaches the virtual depth (≈4× opening price) anyone graduates it — liquidity moves to Raydium at the same price, excess tokens burned, LP burned.
5 · earnsame fee split as auctions: 35% staked holders (+ unused creator share) · 25% RSRCT buyback & burn · 20% verified traders · creator · 10% treasury — until a Raydium graduation, after which Raydium's fees apply.
